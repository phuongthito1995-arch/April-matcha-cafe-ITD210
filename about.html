About the Concept

A café inspired by calm rituals, clean ingredients, and modern comfort.

April Matcha Café is imagined as a small neighborhood destination where people can slow down and enjoy high-quality matcha in a beautiful, peaceful environment.

Brand Journey

How the idea comes to life.

  1. 01

    Create a calm first impression

    The home page opens with a hand-whisked moment, soft typography, and gentle motion to set the mood.

  2. 02

    Share café values and ingredients

    The about page explains where the quality comes from and the rituals behind every drink.

  3. 03

    Showcase drinks, desserts, and experiences

    The menu lays out the full lineup with warm photography and clear descriptions.

  4. 04

    Build atmosphere with the gallery

    A curated grid of café moments helps visitors imagine the texture of being inside.

  5. 05

    Make contact easy and accessible

    A friendly form and clear hours close the experience and invite people in.
